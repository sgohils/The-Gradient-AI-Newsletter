When Tools Fail, Agents Fabricate: Silent Failure and Verification-First Oversight in Tool-Using LLM Agents


What happened: Researchers introduced RetryRealistic, a sandbox that injects realistic faults into eight common tools (filesystem, SQLite, HTTP server, shell, etc.) and observed how tool‑using LLM agents react. The agents often stumble, resorting to naive retries, tool switches, or plan rewrites without guaranteeing success.


Why it matters: In real‑world deployments—automation, data pipelines, or autonomous assistants—silent tool failures can propagate errors or cause downtime. Understanding these failure modes is essential for building verification‑first oversight that catches issues before they cascade.


Key stats: • 9 fault types across 8 tools were tested. • Agents recovered correctly in only ~38% of cases. • Naive retries accounted for 45% of failed recoveries, while proper verification‑first strategies succeeded in >80% of trials.


Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28341
