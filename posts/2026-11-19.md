Google's Gemini API now lets agents tap hundreds of tools while slashing token consumption, entering preview.

## Introducing Tool Retrieval in the Gemini API

**What happened:** Google announced the preview of Tool Retrieval for the Gemini API, enabling AI agents to automatically fetch and invoke the most relevant of hundreds of external tools on demand.

**Why it matters:** By offloading tool selection to the API, developers can build richer agents with far fewer tokens, reducing costs and latency while expanding functional capability.

**Key stats:** • Access to hundreds of tools in a single call
• Token usage reduced by up to 40% in early tests
• Preview availability for all Gemini API users

**Source:** https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-api-tool-retrieval/

## The Gemini app is now available on lower-memory Android devices.

**What happened:** Google announced that the Gemini app now supports lower‑memory Android smartphones, allowing users with as little as 2 GB of RAM to install and use the app. The update adds access to Gemini Live and on‑device custom image generation.

**Why it matters:** This opens AI‑powered chat and image tools to millions of Android users who were previously excluded, boosting adoption and competition in mobile AI.

**Key stats:** • Supports devices with 2 GB RAM or less • Over 1 billion Android devices worldwide, many under this limit • Gemini Live and image creation available at no extra cost.

**Source:** https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/gemini-lower-memory-devices/

## Advancing chip design with OpenAI models

**What happened:** OpenAI’s GPT‑6 Astra was applied to optimize circuits in Jalapeño silicon and to refine RISC‑V core designs.
**Why it matters:** AI‑driven design cuts development cycles, improves performance, and lowers costs, accelerating hardware innovation.
**Key stats:** • 15% power reduction in Jalapeño chips • 12% faster RISC‑V cores • Design cycle shortened by ~30%
**Source:** https://openai.com/index/advancing-chip-design-with-our-models

## Introducing GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol

**What happened:** OpenAI launched the GPT‑6.2 Astra and Sol models, extending the upgrade across ChatGPT Work, Codex, and the public API.

**Why it matters:** The new models aim to deliver more reliable professional text, stronger coding assistance, and better computer‑use integration while cutting compute costs, which could broaden AI adoption in enterprises and developer tools.

**Key stats:** OpenAI says the models provide higher efficiency and lower per‑token pricing, though exact speed‑up and cost‑reduction numbers have not been disclosed.

## Put Gemini to work for you

**What happened:**
Google announced that the Tasks feature in Gemini is now free of charge in six countries. New functionality launches first in the United States, and the app now integrates more deeply with Google Workspace to handle tasks on your behalf.

**Why it matters:**
Free access lowers the barrier for users to adopt AI‑driven productivity tools, while tighter Workspace integration means smoother automation across Gmail, Docs, Calendar, and more, boosting everyday efficiency.

**Key stats:**
- Free in six countries (including the U.S.)
- New U.S. features debut this month
- Expanded Workspace integration covering core productivity apps
