## GPT-6 models demonstrate intrinsic protein-design capabilities

**What happened:** GPT‑6 models were tasked with designing protein structures and amino‑acid sequences using a mix of reasoning prompts and code generation. Laboratory assays confirmed that the AI‑designed proteins exhibited binding activity across five distinct biological targets.

**Why it matters:** This demonstrates a leap from text‑only generation to tangible biotechnological design, potentially accelerating drug discovery, enzyme engineering, and synthetic biology without extensive human iteration.

**Key stats:**
- 5 target proteins successfully bound by AI‑designed candidates
- Designs produced via pure prompting and code, no external datasets needed
- Binding validated in wet‑lab experiments, marking the first empirical proof of AI‑driven protein design at this scale

**What happened:**

**Why it matters:**

**Key stats:**
