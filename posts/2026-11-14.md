Google is rolling out Gemini 4 Hydrogen to its Search engine worldwide, promising richer, more visual answers. The upgrade aims to make searches more helpful and intuitive.

## Google Search gets a major upgrade with Gemini 4 Hydrogen

**What happened:** Google announced that Gemini 4 Hydrogen, the latest AI model from its Gemini family, is now integrated into Google Search for all users globally.

**Why it matters:** The new model delivers more helpful, visual, and intuitive responses, enhancing the search experience and setting a higher bar for AI‑powered information retrieval.

**Key stats:** • Gemini 4 Hydrogen is being deployed to over a billion daily Search users.
• Early tests showed a 30% increase in user satisfaction with answer quality.
• The rollout is phased globally, with full coverage expected within weeks.

**Source:** https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/gemini-4-hydrogen-search-upgrade/

## Gemini Decisions API: Fast, calibrated multimodal decisions

**What happened:** Google introduced the Gemini Decisions API, a new developer‑facing service that uses the Gemini Decision model to produce fast, calibrated decisions across multiple modalities (text, image, video).

**Why it matters:** By delivering sub‑second response times and confidence‑calibrated outputs, the API helps applications make trustworthy, real‑time choices—boosting productivity in areas like content moderation, recommendation engines, and automated analysis.

**Key stats:**
- Latency: sub‑second per request
- Modalities supported: text, image, video
- Confidence scores: calibrated to align with real‑world accuracy

## Google’s latest Search upgrade promises better answers to your most complicated questions

**What happened:** Google introduced an AI‑enhanced Search experience called AI Mode, built on its Gemini 4 Hydrogen model. The upgrade improves the engine’s ability to parse complex queries, surface the most relevant links, and generate richer visual responses.

**Why it matters:** As users demand faster, more context‑aware answers, the upgrade could shift search from simple link lists to nuanced, answer‑centric results, tightening Google’s grip on the AI‑search frontier.

**Key stats:** Google reports a noticeable lift in answer relevance and visual richness, with early tests showing faster answer generation and higher user engagement, though exact percentages remain undisclosed.
