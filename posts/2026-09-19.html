Researchers used Anthropic’s Claude to hack into OpenAI


What happened: Security researchers leveraged Anthropic’s Claude to identify and exploit several undocumented vulnerabilities in OpenAI’s internal tools. Why it matters: The incident underscores how powerful language models can be turned into attack vectors, raising concerns about AI supply‑chain security, cross‑company trust, and the need for stricter model‑usage controls.


Key stats:

  • 3 employee accounts compromised
  • 1 private code repository accessed (≈ 2 GB)
  • Flaws reported within 48 hours of discovery
