Gemini Decisions API: Fast, calibrated multimodal decisions


What happened: Google released the Gemini Decisions API, built on the Gemini Decision model, enabling developers to obtain rapid, calibrated multimodal predictions via a simple API call.

Why it matters: Fast, trustworthy multimodal decisions lower latency bottlenecks in apps like content moderation, recommendation engines, and real‑time analytics, expanding AI’s reach in production environments.

Key stats: • End‑to‑end latency < 100 ms for most requests

• Supports 10+ modalities (text, image, audio, video snippets)

• Calibrated confidence scores with < 2% error in probability estimates

• Free tier includes 1 M decisions per month

Source: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-decisions-api/

Tracing Psychometric Inference in Large Language Models


What happened: Researchers fed 14 LLM variants (Llama 3 and Qwen 2.5, 0.5B‑14B, both base and instruct) a single psychological scale completed by 272 Taiwanese university students, then asked the models to predict scores on six additional scales. Why it matters: If models can accurately infer unseen traits, they may be developing internal representations of human psychology, opening doors for more nuanced AI‑human interaction and raising ethical concerns. Key stats: 14 model versions tested; data from 272 participants; cross‑scale prediction success significantly above chance across most models. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12937
