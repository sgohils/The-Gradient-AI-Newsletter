Google’s latest Gemini model, Omni 1.2 Flash, now supports native 15‑second video creation and editing while delivering stronger output consistency and a 30% price cut. It promises faster, more affordable AI‑powered video workflows for developers and creators.

## Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash: Fast and cost-effective with longer clips

**What happened:** Google unveiled Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, the newest tier of its multimodal AI model, adding native 15‑second video generation and editing capabilities, improved consistency across outputs, and a 30% lower price point compared with the prior version.

**Why it matters:** The upgrade lowers the barrier for developers and content creators to embed high‑quality video AI into apps, speeds up production cycles, and makes large‑scale video generation more cost‑effective, accelerating adoption in marketing, entertainment, and education.

**Key stats:** • 15‑second video generation/editing supported out‑of‑the‑box
• Consistency improvements across text, image, and video outputs
• Pricing reduced by roughly 30% versus Gemini Omni 1.2
• Faster inference latency for real‑time use cases

## GPT‑6 Cyber: Advancing the frontier of aligned cybersecurity agents

**What happened:** OpenAI unveiled GPT‑6 Cyber, an advanced AI system trained to identify security vulnerabilities and propose mitigations, with built‑in alignment controls that keep its actions within authorized scope.

**Why it matters:** Automating vulnerability discovery and fix generation can accelerate threat remediation, lower reliance on manual pen‑testing, and reduce the risk of accidental misuse of powerful AI tools.

**Key stats:** • Built on the GPT‑6 architecture with a dedicated cybersecurity training corpus. • Enhanced alignment layer blocks out‑of‑scope requests, improving safety compliance. • Supports multi‑language code analysis for a broad range of software ecosystems.

## Daybreak Red is now available in AWS GovCloud (US-East)

**Why it matters:** The deployment brings the powerful GPT‑5.6 Cyber model to highly regulated government and enterprise environments, enabling authorized teams to leverage advanced AI for threat detection, analysis, and response while meeting strict compliance requirements.

- Region: AWS GovCloud (US‑East) only
