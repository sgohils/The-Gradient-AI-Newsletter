## Artificiality's First Exam: An LLM-Referenced C-test for Supraintelligence

**What happened:** A team posted a paper (arXiv:2610.14005v1) presenting a C‑test whose items are generated by, and referenced to, large language models (LLMs). The test is framed as an inverse problem—an inductive inference challenge where difficulty scales with the complexity of the underlying mechanism rather than human or existing AI performance.

**Why it matters:** As AI accelerates, traditional benchmarks quickly become saturated or circular because they are calibrated against human or current‑AI abilities. A self‑referential, mechanism‑complexity‑based test could remain meaningful even as machines outstrip their creators, offering a future‑proof yardstick for supraintelligence.

**Key stats:**
- Paper ID: arXiv:2610.14005v1 (new submission)
- Test type: C‑test (cloze‑style) linked to LLM outputs
- Difficulty metric: based on Kolmogorov‑style complexity of the generating mechanism
- No empirical results yet; the work is primarily theoretical.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14005
