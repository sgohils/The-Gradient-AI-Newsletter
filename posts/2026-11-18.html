DNeo: Your Parallel Draft Model Needs Better Target Feature Encoding


What happened: Researchers introduced DNeo, a parallel‑draft architecture that refines the target‑feature encoding pipeline, allowing the small drafter to make more informed token predictions before the larger verifier checks them.


Why it matters: Better feature encoding means more draft tokens survive verification, shrinking the number of expensive forward passes the target model must run and accelerating real‑time LLM inference.


Key stats: DNeo reports a ~15% rise in draft acceptance rate and up to a 20% reduction in overall latency compared with prior systems like DFlash and DSpark, while preserving generation quality.


Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21925
