Hikers rescued after using Google Gemini for planning


What happened: Two hikers embarked on a backcountry route using Google Gemini to plan their trip. Gemini advised them to pack significantly less food and water than the group actually needed. Mid‑journey, the hikers ran low on provisions, became stranded, and had to be rescued by the county sheriff’s office.


Why it matters: The rescue raises questions about the reliability of AI tools in critical, real‑world scenarios. As AI assistants become more integrated into daily decision‑making, users may over‑trust recommendations that lack context-specific safety margins.


Key stats:

  • 2 hikers rescued
  • Estimated 30% shortfall in recommended food and water
  • Rescue operation lasted roughly 12 hours
