## Better prompt caching for GPT-6

**What happened:** GPT‑6 upgrades its prompt‑caching engine, delivering higher cache‑hit rates, new diagnostics, explicit breakpoints, and fine‑grained controls.
**Why it matters:** Faster responses and reduced compute translate into lower expenses for developers and a smoother user experience across applications.
**Key stats:** • Cache hit rate up 35% vs. GPT‑5
• Latency cut by up to 40%
• Cost per token reduced by ~25%

**What happened:** OpenAI announced GPT-6 Sol, a high‑capacity model, and GPT-6 Luna, a budget‑friendly alternative, expanding its AI portfolio for diverse workloads.

## The AI Neuroscientist: An Interactive Agentic Interface for Neuroimaging Analysis

**What happened:** Researchers unveiled the AI Neuroscientist, a conversational agent that couples a large language model (LLM) with a full neuroimaging toolkit. The system lets users run quality‑control checks, statistical modeling, and visualizations simply by typing queries.

**Why it matters:** By removing the need for specialized coding, the tool opens advanced neuroimaging analysis to researchers without deep computational backgrounds, accelerating hypothesis testing and fostering interdisciplinary collaboration.

**Key stats:** • Integrates an LLM with neuroimaging modules for QC, modeling, and visualization.
• Enables end‑to‑end analysis through natural‑language prompts.
• Presented as a new arXiv preprint (2609.25254v1).

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25254

[Read more](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25254)

## MedGate-Fusion: Integrating First-Encounter Semantic Narratives and Physiological Biomarkers for Prospective Stroke Risk Stratification

**What happened:** Researchers introduced MedGate-Fusion, a multi‑modal gated neural network that fuses transformer‑derived embeddings of first‑encounter clinical narratives with ten common physiological risk markers.

**Why it matters:** Early stroke signals are scattered across unstructured notes and lab results, making them hard to detect in primary care. By uniting both data streams, the model promises more accurate prospective risk stratification, potentially enabling earlier interventions.

**Key stats:** • Integrates 10 routinely recorded biomarkers.
• Uses transformer embeddings of free‑text encounter notes.
• Trained on electronic medical records from the Canadian Primary Care Sentinel Surveillance Network.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25272
