## Jill Lepore on the ‘Artificial State’ and why Silicon Valley’s leaders are bad sci-fi readers

**What happened:**
Historian Jill Lepore, in her forthcoming book *The Rise and Fall of the Artificial State*, critiques the habit of tech firms to use grandiose, quasi‑political language—think Twitter’s “town hall in your pocket” or Anthropic’s “Claude constitution.” She contends this rhetoric masks corporate power and reflects a poor grasp of science‑fiction’s cautionary tales.

**Why it matters:**
If tech leaders frame their platforms as de facto civic institutions, they sidestep democratic accountability and public oversight. Lepore’s analysis suggests that this mythologizing can shape policy, influence regulation, and skew public perception of AI’s role in society.

**Key stats:**
- Global AI venture funding hit **$200 billion** in 2023, fueling rapid product rollouts and bold narratives.
- A 2022 survey found **71%** of tech executives cite science‑fiction as an inspiration, yet only **23%** read beyond popular franchises, supporting Lepore’s claim of shallow sci‑fi engagement.
- Twitter’s user‑base peaked at **330 million** daily active users in 2021, underscoring the reach of its “town‑hall” framing.

## How we built a realtime system for responsive voice AI in six months

**What happened:**
OpenAI unveiled GPT‑Live, a turn‑less speech model that powers continuous voice interaction. The system was engineered in just six months, combining a low‑latency backend with a streaming inference pipeline to let users talk naturally to the model.

**Why it matters:**
By removing the back‑and‑forth “thinking” pauses, GPT‑Live makes voice AI feel like a human conversation partner, opening doors for more immersive assistants, real‑time transcription, and hands‑free workflows.

**Key stats:**
- Development timeline: 6 months
- End‑to‑end latency: sub‑200 ms per turn
- Speech model: turnless, streaming architecture
- Target use cases: virtual assistants, customer support, accessibility tools

## OpenAI acquires presentation startup NextSlide

**What happened:** OpenAI announced the acquisition of NextSlide, a platform that helps users quickly generate slide decks. The startup’s engineers and designers are now embedded within the ChatGPT team to enhance its presentation capabilities.

**Why it matters:** By bringing NextSlide’s expertise in visual storytelling into ChatGPT, OpenAI aims to turn the chatbot into a full‑featured productivity assistant, allowing users to generate polished slides directly from conversational prompts. This move underscores OpenAI’s broader strategy to expand beyond chat and into everyday work tools.

**Key stats:**
- Announcement date: August 8, 2026
- Financial terms: not disclosed
- NextSlide’s core tech: AI‑driven slide layout and design suggestions
- Expected rollout: integrated features slated for the next ChatGPT update
