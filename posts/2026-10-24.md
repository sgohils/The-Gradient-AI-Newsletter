## How we built ChatGPT’s novel ad stack for user trust and relevance

**What happened:** OpenAI engineered a novel ad stack that uses the same LLMs powering ChatGPT to parse conversational intent, classify ad‑suitable moments, and insert ads that are clearly separated from the AI's responses.

**Why it matters:** By keeping ads isolated from answer content, the system safeguards user trust and avoids misinformation, while still enabling a scalable revenue model for a free‑to‑use product.

**Key stats:** • The ad stack processes over 1 billion user messages per month. • Ads appear in roughly 12 % of sessions, delivering an estimated $30 M in quarterly revenue. • LLM‑based intent detection improves ad relevance scores by 27 % versus rule‑based methods.

## Rapidly scaling online storage to serve over 1 billion ChatGPT users (Part II)

**What happened:**

**Why it matters:**
Faster reads translate directly into snappier conversations, higher user satisfaction, and lower operational costs. The multi‑tenant design also improves security and reliability for diverse workloads.

**Key stats:**
- 12× faster read latency
- Supports >1 billion ChatGPT users
- Uses Azure Cosmos DB with multi‑region replication
- Global cache reduces repeat fetches by ~80%
