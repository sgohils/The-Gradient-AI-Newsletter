## Asana cuts model costs 76x in browser tests with GPT-6.1 Sol

**What happened:** Asana used GPT-6 Astra in Codex to optimize a browser-agent workflow running on GPT-6.1 Sol. In its tests, the optimized workflow was 76 times cheaper and five times faster than its original production setup.

**Why it matters:** Lower costs and faster response times make it feasible for Asana to roll out more capable AI features to customers, potentially expanding the adoption of AI‑driven productivity tools.

**Key stats:** • Cost reduction: 76× cheaper • Performance gain: 5× faster • Optimized workflow: GPT-6.1 Sol; experiments: GPT-6 Astra in Codex
