## TACS-RF: Tool-driven Agentic Circuit Synthesis of RF and mmWave Modules

**What happened:**
The team introduced TACS‑RF, a tool‑driven agentic circuit synthesis platform. An LLM coding agent (Claude Opus 4.8) orchestrates Cadence Virtuoso/Spectre, Calibre, Cadence EMX, and Ansys HFSS to design RF and mmWave modules, demonstrably producing a 240‑251 GHz sub‑THz transmitter.

**Why it matters:**
Automating the complex RF/mmWave design loop with AI cuts design time, reduces manual errors, and opens rapid prototyping of next‑gen communication hardware, potentially accelerating 6G and satellite‑link development.

**Key stats:**
- LLM used: Claude Opus 4.8
- EDA tools integrated: Cadence Virtuoso/Spectre, Calibre, Cadence EMX, Ansys HFSS
- Demonstrated frequency range: 240–251 GHz (sub‑THz)
- Design workflow fully autonomous from specification to layout

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31882
