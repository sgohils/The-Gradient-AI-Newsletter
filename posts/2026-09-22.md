## Attention-Aware Routing: Coupling Routing and Attention in MoEs

**What happened:** Researchers introduced AAR, which feeds temporal and spectral features from a sliding window of attention weights into the MoE router, supplementing the token's hidden state.
**Why it matters:** By tapping into the model's own attention dynamics, AAR offers a richer, context‑aware expert selection, potentially improving language understanding and generation without redesigning the transformer backbone.
**Key stats:** The paper (arXiv:2609.20974v1) reports that AAR enhances routing decisions and yields measurable performance gains on benchmark tasks, all while keeping the base transformer architecture unchanged.
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20974

## CaLR: Causal Latent Revision for Robust Diffusion Reasoning

**What happened:** Researchers presented Causal Latent Revision (CaLR), a method that treats reasoning as constrained latent optimization, using a causal topology matrix from an expert model and implicit differentiation to guide diffusion language models.

**Why it matters:** It tackles the local greediness of autoregressive models and the weak causal structure of diffusion models, promising more reliable and interpretable AI reasoning.

**Key stats:** The study reports measurable improvements on standard reasoning benchmarks, including up to a 10% accuracy gain over baseline diffusion language models.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20981
