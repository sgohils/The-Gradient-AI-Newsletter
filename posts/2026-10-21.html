Navalia: Advancing Cowork Agents through Verifiable Task Synthesis and Long-Horizon Post-Training


What happened: Researchers unveiled Navalia, a system that automatically synthesizes verifiable, long‑horizon tasks for large‑language‑model (LLM) Cowork Agents. It combines cross‑document evidence gathering with multi‑step numerical reasoning, reducing the need for costly expert authoring.


Why it matters: Financial analysis demands precise, multi‑source reasoning. Navalia’s approach lets agents plan autonomously and verify their answers, closing the gap between conversational assistants and fully fledged autonomous analysts.


Key stats: • Navalia generates tasks that integrate evidence from multiple documents and require multi‑step calculations.

• In benchmark evaluations, agents trained with Navalia‑crafted tasks outperformed those using manually authored or naïvely synthesized tasks.

• The framework supports post‑training fine‑tuning for horizons beyond ten reasoning steps.


Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26887
